French Polynesia Fish ID ("the app") is a field guide to the fish of French Polynesia. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your quiz progress is saved only on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

If the app offers an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Third-party content

Photos are used under Creative Commons licences or are in the public domain, and each photo's author and licence are shown in the app. Tapping a credit or source link opens that website in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
