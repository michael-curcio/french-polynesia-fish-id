Common questions

Does the app work without signal? Yes. Every fish card, photo and quiz is built into the app, so it works fully offline, including at sea.

I bought the unlock on another device. How do I get it back? Open the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

I think a fish ID or rule is wrong. Please email us with the fish name and what you saw; corrections are very welcome.

Important

This app is a learning aid, not legal or medical advice. Fishing rules and protected species lists change; check current French Polynesia regulations (Direction des ressources marines) before you dive, and ask locals about ciguatera before eating reef fish.
