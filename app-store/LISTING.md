## Description (4000)
French Polynesia Fish ID is a fast, offline field guide for spearfishers and freedivers in French Polynesia.

Swipe through the fish by habitat, from blue water and drop-offs to reef predators, shallow surge zones, and sharks and rays. Every card gives you what matters in the water:

• A clear verdict: Trophy Target, Shoot, Avoid or Illegal
• Typical size, depth, spearfishing world record and hunting difficulty
• How to hunt it and where to place the shot
• Key ID features and the look-alikes to watch for
• Where you'll find it
• Eating quality and ciguatera risk

TROPHY TARGETS
A dedicated deck of the fish spearos prize most, including dogtooth tuna, yellowfin tuna, giant trevally, wahoo, mahi-mahi and amberjack, ordered by spearfishing world record.

KNOW WHAT'S PROTECTED
All sharks, rays and turtles are protected in French Polynesia. The app flags every protected species and what to do when you meet one.

LEARN IT BEFORE YOU GO
• Fish ID quiz: name the fish from the photo
• Fish Info quiz: rules, safety, eating and hunting questions
Your progress is saved on your device.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
Everything is built into the app, so it works at sea, on the boat and on remote islands.

Photos are used under Creative Commons licences or are public domain, with every photographer credited in the app.

This app is a learning aid, not legal or medical advice. Always check current French Polynesia fishing regulations and ask locals about ciguatera before eating reef fish.
